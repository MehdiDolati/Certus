# Certus Business Goals

## Purpose

This document defines the business objectives that guide Certus development.

While the product vision describes the long-term purpose and direction of
Certus, business goals define the measurable outcomes required to validate
Certus as a viable quantitative trading organization.

---

# Strategic Business Objective

The initial strategic objective of Certus is to establish a sustainable
quantitative trading operation by developing, validating, and operating
systematic trading strategies through a disciplined research process.

The first milestone is not to build a large organization or a fully
autonomous system.

The first milestone is to prove that Certus can create measurable value
through a repeatable quantitative research workflow.

---

# Phase 0: Foundation and Validation

## Objective

Build the minimum Quantitative Research Operating System required to conduct
repeatable, documented, and disciplined trading research.

## Expected Outcomes

- Establish a structured research workflow
- Track quantitative experiments
- Record hypotheses and results
- Build a reusable knowledge base
- Reduce the time between idea generation and validation
- Create the foundation for future AI-agent integration

## Success Indicators

- Research experiments follow a consistent process
- Results are reproducible
- Failed experiments create reusable knowledge
- Strategy development becomes measurable and comparable

---

# Phase 1: Trading Capability Validation

## Objective

Demonstrate that Certus can support the discovery, validation, and operation
of systematic trading strategies.

## Expected Outcomes

- Develop a portfolio of tested strategies
- Establish realistic performance evaluation methods
- Create a track record of research decisions
- Identify repeatable sources of quantitative edge

## Success Indicators

- Strategies survive validation criteria
- Performance is evaluated with realistic assumptions
- Risk management principles are applied consistently
- Research decisions are documented and explainable

---

# Phase 2: Sustainable Operation

## Objective

Transform Certus from an experimental system into a sustainable quantitative
operation.

## Expected Outcomes

- Operate validated strategies continuously
- Improve research efficiency through AI assistance
- Build stronger strategy portfolios
- Reduce dependence on manual research activities

## Success Indicators

- Consistent research output
- Increasing automation of repetitive workflows
- Growing knowledge base
- Improved research-to-deployment cycle time

---

# Phase 3: AI-Native Quantitative Organization

## Objective

Evolve Certus into an AI-native quantitative organization where humans and
specialized AI agents collaborate across the entire strategy lifecycle.

## Expected Outcomes

- Multi-agent research workflows
- Automated experiment management
- Advanced validation processes
- Continuous learning from historical research

## Success Indicators

- Agents successfully support specialized research roles
- Human researchers focus on high-value decisions
- The system continuously improves through accumulated knowledge

---

# Business Model Evolution

Certus may evolve through multiple value creation paths:

## Internal Quantitative Operation

Using Certus capabilities to manage internal trading strategies.

## Research Platform

Providing AI-assisted quantitative research capabilities to independent
traders and small teams.

## Quantitative Research Services

Offering AI-augmented research capabilities to organizations requiring
systematic trading workflows.

The initial priority is not selecting a final business model, but building
the underlying quantitative research capability.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Build Capability Before Scale

Certus should first prove its ability to create value before expanding
users, capital, or infrastructure.

## Evidence Before Investment

Major decisions should be supported by measurable results and validated
experiments.

## Process Is the Product

The long-term competitive advantage of Certus is the ability to repeatedly
generate, test, and improve quantitative knowledge.

## Incremental Autonomy

Automation and AI-agent capabilities should be introduced gradually based
on demonstrated value.

---

# Current Priority

The immediate priority of Certus is:

> Build the foundation required to transform quantitative trading research
> from an individual activity into a structured, measurable, and continuously
> improving process.
