# Certus Product Vision

## Vision Statement

Certus aims to build an AI-native quantitative research organization that
brings institutional-grade research capabilities to independent traders
and small quantitative teams.

Through collaborative AI agents, systematic experimentation, knowledge
accumulation, rigorous validation, and risk-aware workflows, Certus enables
the discovery, development, and evolution of quantitative trading strategies
across financial markets.

---

## Problem Statement

Professional quantitative trading organizations benefit from large teams
of researchers, engineers, data specialists, and risk managers working
together through structured research processes.

Independent traders and small teams usually lack access to:

- systematic research workflows
- scalable experimentation processes
- reliable validation frameworks
- institutional-grade knowledge management
- continuous improvement mechanisms

As a result, many trading ideas are developed through fragmented,
manual, and inconsistent processes, making it difficult to distinguish
real market opportunities from overfitting and random patterns.

Certus exists to reduce this gap.

---

## Opportunity

Recent advances in artificial intelligence, particularly autonomous and
collaborative AI agents, create an opportunity to replicate parts of the
research capabilities traditionally available only to institutional
quantitative firms.

Rather than replacing quantitative researchers, Certus aims to augment
human capabilities by delegating repetitive, analytical, and knowledge-heavy
tasks to specialized AI agents.

Examples include:

- market research
- hypothesis generation
- experiment planning
- result analysis
- documentation
- knowledge retrieval
- research workflow management

---

## Target Users

### Primary Users

Independent quantitative traders and small trading teams who want to apply
a more systematic and research-driven approach to strategy development.

### Future Users

- small proprietary trading firms
- quantitative researchers
- investment organizations seeking AI-assisted research capabilities

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Research Before Trading

Every trading strategy starts as a hypothesis.

Certus treats trading ideas as research questions that require evidence,
experimentation, and validation before deployment.

---

### 2. Knowledge Is the Primary Asset

The long-term value of Certus is not individual trading signals.

The primary asset is accumulated quantitative knowledge:

- tested hypotheses
- experiment results
- failed approaches
- validated strategies
- market insights
- decision history

---

### 3. Progressive Autonomy

Certus evolves gradually from human-supervised workflows toward higher
levels of AI autonomy.

Early stages prioritize:

- human judgment
- transparent processes
- explainable decisions
- controlled experimentation

Automation and autonomy increase as confidence and validation improve.

---

### 4. Validation Over Optimization

Certus prioritizes robust validation over maximizing backtest performance.

The system should favor:

- realistic assumptions
- out-of-sample validation
- risk awareness
- reproducibility

over:

- curve fitting
- unrealistic optimization
- short-term performance chasing

---

## Strategic Scope

Certus is designed as a market-agnostic quantitative research platform.

The initial implementation focuses on Forex markets because of:

- availability of historical data
- accessibility for independent research
- lower infrastructure requirements
- suitability for systematic experimentation

The architecture is designed to support additional financial markets
including:

- equities
- futures
- commodities
- digital assets
- other quantitative investment domains

---

## Long-Term Ambition

Certus aims to become an AI-native quantitative investment organization
and a foundational platform for the next generation of quantitative traders.

The long-term ambition is to create a continuously learning quantitative
ecosystem where AI agents, human researchers, and accumulated knowledge
work together to discover and operate systematic investment strategies.

---

## Initial Focus

The first phase of Certus focuses on building the minimum quantitative
research operating system required to run repeatable and disciplined
trading experiments.

Initial priorities:

- research workflow management
- experiment tracking
- strategy lifecycle management
- backtest result analysis
- knowledge accumulation
- human-supervised AI assistance

The first trading strategies may be developed manually using existing tools
such as StrategyQuant X, while Certus gradually introduces AI assistance
into the research lifecycle.

---

## Non-Goals

Certus does not aim to:

- guarantee trading profits
- perfectly predict financial markets
- immediately replace human decision-making
- build high-frequency or low-latency trading systems in early phases
- optimize strategies solely based on historical backtest performance
- create a black-box trading system without explainability or validation

---

## Success Criteria

Early success for Certus means:

- establishing a repeatable quantitative research process
- reducing the time required from idea to validated experiment
- building a growing knowledge base of quantitative research
- developing and operating validated systematic strategies
- demonstrating that AI-assisted workflows can improve quantitative research capability
